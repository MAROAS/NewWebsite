DODO Max Australia – Driver & Vehicle Requirements

Driver Requirements – VIC

  • Have a smartphone, mobile number, and email address
  • Be an Australian citizen, permanent resident, or have the right to work in Australia
  • Held an unrestricted Australian driver licence for at least 6 months
  • Have an ABN and be GST-registered
  • Hold a current Driver Accreditation
  • Be listed as the insured driver on the insurance

Vehicle Requirements – VIC

  • No more than 15 years old
  • Minimum of 4 doors
  • Seats between 4–6 passengers
  • RWC accepted (exempt for new vehicles within 12 months)
  • Covered under current third-party or comprehensive insurance
  • Registered as a Commercial Passenger Vehicle in Victoria

Driver Requirements – NSW

  • Have a smartphone, mobile number, and email address
  • Be an Australian citizen, permanent resident, or have the right to work in Australia
  • Held an unrestricted Australian driver licence for at least 12 months
  • Have an ABN and be GST-registered
  • No serious criminal offences
  • No serious traffic offences
  • Be listed as the insured driver on the insurance

Vehicle Requirements – NSW

  • No more than 15 years old
  • Minimum of 4 doors
  • Seats between 4–6 passengers
  • Not an ex-taxi
  • Passed a pink slip inspection within the last 12 months (exempt for new vehicles within 12 months)
  • Registered in NSW and covered by NSW CTP insurance
  • Covered under current third-party or comprehensive insurance

Driver Requirements – QLD

  • Have a smartphone, mobile number, and email address
  • Be an Australian citizen, permanent resident, or have the right to work in Australia
  • Held an unrestricted Australian driver licence for at least 12 months
  • Have an ABN and be GST-registered
  • Hold a Driver Authority (Type BHTX, TAXI, or LIMO)
  • Be listed as the insured driver on the insurance
  • Complete mandatory driver training

Vehicle Requirements – QLD

  • No more than 15 years old
  • Minimum of 4 doors
  • Seats between 4–6 passengers
  • Not an ex-taxi
  • Passed inspection within the last 12 months (exempt for new vehicles within 12 months)
  • Registered in QLD and covered by QLD CTP insurance
  • Covered under current third-party or comprehensive insurance
  • Hold a Booked Hire Service Licence (BHSL)

Driver Requirements – WA

  • Have a smartphone, mobile number, and email address
  • Be an Australian citizen, permanent resident, or have the right to work in Australia
  • Held an unrestricted Australian driver licence for at least 12 months
  • Have an ABN and be GST-registered
  • Hold a Passenger Transport Driver Authorisation (PTD-A)
  • Be listed as the insured driver on the insurance

Vehicle Requirements – WA

  • No more than 15 years old
  • Minimum of 4 doors
  • Seats between 4–6 passengers
  • Not an ex-taxi
  • Registered in WA and covered by 3F Motor Injury Insurance (MII)
  • Covered under current third-party or comprehensive insurance
  • Registered for Passenger Transport Vehicle Authorisation (PTV-A)

Driver Requirements – SA

  • Have a smartphone, mobile number, and email address
  • Be an Australian citizen, permanent resident, or have the right to work in Australia
  • Held an unrestricted Australian driver licence for at least 12 months
  • Have an ABN and be GST-registered
  • Hold a current Driver Accreditation
  • Hold a valid Operator Accreditation
  • Be listed as the insured driver on the insurance

Vehicle Requirements – SA

  • No more than 8 years old
  • Minimum of 4 doors
  • Seats between 4–6 passengers
  • Not an ex-taxi
  • Passed inspection within the last 12 months
  • Covered under upgraded CTP insurance (class: 48, 98, 7, 58)
  • Covered under current third-party or comprehensive insurance
