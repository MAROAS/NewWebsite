1. Introduction

DODO TRANSPORT PTY LTD (“DODO”, “we”, “us” or “our”) provides technology connecting people requesting transportation services (Riders) with independent drivers providing transportation services (Drivers). ABN: 37 690 944 177, Australia. Last updated: 26 October 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how DODO collects, holds, uses, discloses and protects personal information when you use the DODO Rider application, DODO Driver application, website, apply as a Driver, take a trip, contact support, or interact with DODO.

DODO is committed to handling personal information consistently with applicable Australian privacy law, including the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

Some information is necessary to provide services (account creation, booking dispatch, Driver verification, trip communication).

2. What Personal Information DODO Collects

2.1 Account and contact information: Full name, email address, mobile telephone number, address, account identifiers, profile photograph, preferences, authentication records, and status.

2.2 Driver information: Driver’s licence, booked-hire authorisation, identity documents, vehicle registration, make/model/year/colour/plate, vehicle inspection (COI), compulsory third party and motor insurance, accreditation, onboarding status, and, where required for the Driver's authorisation, background check results.

2.3 Trip and booking information: Pickup, destination, intermediate stops, coordinates, route, distance, duration, ETAs, Driver live location while available or en route, timestamps, fares, tolls, tips, cancellations, ratings, and transaction history.

3. Location Information

Location data is collected while searching/requesting trips, travelling to pickup, completing trips, navigating, tracking Driver approach, and while a Driver is online and available.

Drivers may need to permit background location processing while online or during an active trip so riders and dispatch systems receive current location.

4. Device and Technical Information

We collect device type, OS and version, app version, IP address, network details, diagnostic logs, crash reports, performance information, and session security events.

5. Device Permissions

  • Location — Used for bookings, navigation, Driver tracking, pickup/destination matching, safety and fraud prevention.
  • Camera and photo library — Used in the DODO Driver app to take or upload a profile photo, identity documents and vehicle certificates for verification.
  • Microphone — The DODO apps do not use your microphone.
  • Notifications — Used for trip requests, dispatch updates, booking events, earnings, support and safety alerts.
  • Phone functionality — Used to trigger phone dialler for masked, privacy-protected calling between Rider and Driver.

6. Rider and Driver Communications

Private Call Masking: Where available, DODO uses a call-masking service (via Twilio infrastructure) so Riders and Drivers can communicate without displaying their private phone numbers to each other.

Masked telephone numbers are routing numbers and are not public customer-support lines.

DODO customer support is reachable at 0415 152 099.

7. Messages and Communications Records

Communications sent through DODO may be retained where reasonably necessary to provide support, investigate disputes or safety incidents, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations.

DODO does not record the audio content of ordinary telephone calls unless such functionality is specifically implemented with required consent.

8. Payment and Financial Information

DODO uses specialist payment providers (including Stripe) to process payments securely. Complete payment card numbers are handled directly by payment providers.

DODO receives tokenised identifiers, last 4 digits, payment statuses, and transaction histories to calculate driver earnings, tips, tolls and refunds.

9. Ratings, Feedback, Complaints and Safety

We collect ratings, compliments, complaints, incident reports, evidence voluntarily supplied, lost property claims, and safety reports to maintain platform quality and safety.

10–11. Sensitive Information & Biometrics

Sensitive information (for example criminal history check status, accessibility needs, or health information in an incident report) is collected only where the law allows it and, where required, with your consent, and access to it is restricted.

Biometrics: DODO does not currently collect biometric information, such as facial images for identity verification. If DODO introduces biometric verification, we will tell the people affected before it starts and ask for their consent where the law requires it.

If you choose to unlock the DODO Driver app with your phone's fingerprint or face unlock, that check happens on your phone. DODO does not receive your fingerprint or face data.

12–14. Collection, Use & Sharing

Collected directly from you, automatically via app use, from other users (ratings/complaints), and authorised verification providers.

Used to administer accounts, match riders and drivers, navigate routes, process payments, verify compliance, detect fraud, and comply with transport and safety laws.

Limited information is shared between Rider and Driver for trip coordination (first name, photo, rating, vehicle details, live approach location). Private phone numbers remain masked.

15. Automated Systems and Decision-Making

Some decisions are made automatically by our systems:

  • matching a Rider's request with an available nearby Driver, using the pickup location and Drivers' locations and availability;
  • calculating the fare shown before a trip, using the trip's distance and estimated time; and
  • pausing a Driver's ability to go online after 12 hours online in a day, using the Driver's online time.

If you think an automated decision about you is wrong, call 0415 152 099 and a member of our team will review it.

16–17. Cookies, Web & Marketing

The DODO apps and website use device and browser storage for security and to make features work. For example, the website keeps a temporary identifier in your browser's session storage so a form is not submitted twice. The DODO website does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

We send direct marketing only where you have agreed to receive it. Every marketing message includes a way to unsubscribe, and we act on unsubscribe requests within the time required by the Spam Act 2003 (Cth).

18–20. Service Providers, Overseas Disclosure & Authorities

Service providers that handle personal information for DODO include Supabase (database and app hosting), Stripe (payments), Twilio (call masking, where available) and Google (maps in the DODO apps).

Our website. When you visit www.dodotransport.com.au, your browser connects to the following services. They receive your device's IP address and basic browser information as part of normal web requests:

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts (Google) supplies the website's typeface.
  • jsDelivr supplies the map software (MapLibre) used on the home page.
  • OpenFreeMap supplies the map images on the home page, based on OpenStreetMap data.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim looks up the street address of a point you choose. It is used only if you tap the map or the location button. Your device's location is used only if you tap the location button and allow it in your browser.
  • Website forms (pre-booking, contact and driver application), when they are open, send your details to DODO's systems hosted by Supabase, and confirmation emails are sent through Resend.
  • Online booking form at old.dodotransport.com.au, when available: your details are emailed to DODO through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form service. Its own booking privacy notice applies.
  • TikTok receives information only if you follow our link to TikTok.

Overseas. DODO's main database is hosted by Supabase in South Korea (Amazon Web Services, Seoul region). Several other service providers, including Stripe, Twilio, Google, GitHub and Resend, are based in the United States and may handle information there or in other countries where they operate. Where we disclose personal information overseas, we take the steps required by Australian Privacy Principle 8.

We disclose personal information to government bodies where the law requires it, including:

  • the Queensland Department of Transport and Main Roads: booking records when requested, regular reports containing details of Drivers, vehicles and trips, and a notice if we end a Driver's arrangement because of serious safety misconduct; and
  • the Australian Taxation Office: twice-yearly reports of Drivers' identity, ABN and payment details under the Sharing Economy Reporting Regime.

We may also disclose information to police, courts or insurers where the law requires or permits it, including for serious safety investigations.

21–23. Storage, Security, Data Breaches & Retention

We protect personal information with security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) to our apps, website and database services, access controls, role-based permissions and database access rules.

If a data breach is likely to result in serious harm, we will notify the affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy or as the law requires. For example, Queensland law requires booking records to be kept for at least 2 years, and tax and financial records must be kept for the periods required by tax and corporations law. When we no longer need information, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it. A deletion request does not override a legal duty to keep records.

24–27. Access, Correction, Choices & Account Deletion

Under APPs 12 & 13, you may request access to and correction of your personal information.

Users can update details in-app or request account deletion, subject to resolving active trips and legal/tax retention requirements.

28–29. Changes, Complaints & Privacy Officer Contact

Policy updates will be published with a revised effective date.
