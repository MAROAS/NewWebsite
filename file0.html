Requirements

What you'll need in Queensland

You'll upload these documents in the DODO Driver app. DODO reviews them before you can go online.

Driving outside Queensland? DODO is launching in Queensland first. We’ll confirm what your state needs before trips open there.

Before you apply: you'll need a smartphone, a mobile number and an email address, and you must be at least 21 years old.

Driver · Queensland

  • Queensland Driver's Authorisation (DA badge)
  • Valid Queensland driver licence (front & back)
  • National Police Check certificate (issued in the last 12 months)
  • Queensland driving history statement
  • Australian Business Number (ABN), registered for GST
  • Training modules: anti-touting, driver safety, preventing conflict, and assistance animals
  • Acceptance of the DODO Driver Partner Terms

Vehicle · Queensland

  • Booked Hire Service Licence (BHSL)
  • Vehicle registration matching the vehicle's plate
  • Certificate of Inspection (annual safety inspection)
  • Vehicle insurance certificate (comprehensive or third-party commercial rideshare policy)

Driver application

Tell us a little about you and we'll be in touch about next steps.

DODO currently takes trips in Queensland only. Drivers in other states can register now, but can’t accept trips until DODO is approved to operate in that state and has approved you for it. Where you live doesn’t count as approval.

Please don't type licence numbers or attach documents here. If we take your application further, you'll upload your licence, accreditation and vehicle documents securely in the DODO Driver app.
