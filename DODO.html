How it works

Ride in three steps

Simple, transparent and on your schedule.

1

Set your trip

Enter your pickup and destination, then pick the ride type that suits you.

2

See your fare upfront

Your fare is worked out from distance and time and shown before you confirm.

3

Get matched & go

We match you with the nearest driver, and your card is charged securely through Stripe when the trip ends.

Trust

Clear fares, secure payments

Know what you'll pay before you ride, and pay safely in the app.

  • Your fare is shown before you request a ride
  • Card payments are processed securely by Stripe
  • Optional tips go 100% to your driver
  • Drivers need an approved profile photo and vehicle before they can go online
