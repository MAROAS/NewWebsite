About DODO

DODO Transport is a rideshare company based in Queensland. We are preparing to launch the DODO Rider and Driver apps in Queensland.

Six ride options

DODO Priority, DODO X, DODO Economy, DODO Comfort, DODO BUSINESS and DODO Premium. See the services.

Fare shown before you book

You see what you'll pay before you ride, and you pay by card in the app. Tips go 100% to drivers.

Drivers who meet Queensland's requirements

Drivers apply with their documents and vehicle details, and DODO checks them before they can take trips. Become a driver.
