Ride with DODO

How DODO works

Booking a ride takes a few taps in the DODO Rider app.

  1. 1

    Set your trip

    Enter your pickup and destination.

  2. 2

    Choose your ride

    Pick DODO X or DODO Priority and see your fare before you request.

  3. 3

    Track your driver

    Follow your driver to your pickup. Cancelling before your trip starts is free.

  4. 4

    Pay by card

    Your card is authorised when you request and charged when your trip ends.

Why DODO

Simple, clear and secure

Clear fares

Your fare is shown upfront, before you request your ride.

You choose toll roads

Routes are toll-free unless you choose toll roads. If you do, the quoted toll is shown with your total before you confirm, and it will not increase.

Driver tracking

See your driver’s progress to your pickup and know when they have arrived.

Secure card payments

Card payments are processed by Stripe. DODO does not store your full card number.

Drive with DODO

Drive on your schedule

Register in the DODO Driver app. DODO reviews your documents, and you can go online once you are approved.

  • Submit your driver licence, vehicle, insurance and the accreditation required in your state.
  • Add an approved profile photo and vehicle.
  • Go online and accept trips once DODO approves your account.
